Cognitive Science senior project · Lehigh University · 2026–27

Who the Model Learns to Fund

Venture investors increasingly use AI to screen startups. If that AI learns what a winner looks like from past exits, and its picks become the next exits, does it lock historical bias into who gets funded? And where could the loop be broken?

The cognitive science underneath it is prototype theory: people, and models trained on people's choices, judge a candidate by how closely it resembles past examples.
